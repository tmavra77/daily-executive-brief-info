Scope

Daily Executive Brief is a personal utility operated by tmavra77. It is intended to summarize the owner's Notion tasks and primary Google Calendar and send the result to the owner's fixed Gmail address. There is no public registration, paid subscription or service offered to other account holders.

Authorized accounts

The utility is intended for accounts and data sources the owner is authorized to use. Access requires the relevant Google consent and a read-only Notion connection. The owner controls these permissions and can revoke them at the providers.

Briefing limits

The brief is an informational aid. It depends on source data, provider availability and scheduled execution. Dates, omissions or delays should be checked against the original calendar and task database when timing matters. Delivery time is approximate.

Provider services and costs

The utility does not charge a subscription. Google, Notion and GitHub accounts remain subject to their own terms, plans and usage limits. This notice does not modify any agreement with those providers.

Privacy and stopping use

The privacy notice describes information access, processing, storage and deletion controls. The owner can stop the utility by disabling its scheduled workflow and revoking its provider authorizations.
